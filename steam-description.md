[h1]Description of Mod[/h1]
[b]Better Pawn Control Forked[/b] is a bulk policy manager for RimWorld. It lets you save and switch presets for outfits, schedules, work, animals, mechs, and other supported pawn-control integrations from the normal game tabs.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Policy Presets:[/b] Save and switch presets for outfits, food, drugs, reading, medicine, hostility response, schedules, and work.
[*][b]Animal and Mech Support:[/b] Manage animal, mech, robot, and supported loadout presets from the relevant tabs.
[*][b]Default Policies:[/b] Set default policies for new colonists, prisoners, and slaves.
[*][b]Emergency Toggle:[/b] Apply your configured policy set quickly when you need a fast colony-wide change.
[*][b]Integration Support:[/b] Includes compatibility for Work Tab, Animal Tab, Combat Extended, and other supported mods.
[*][b]Safe Compatibility Behavior:[/b] Avoids interrupting Defensive Positions Forked and Outfit Stands Plus Forked flows when those mods are present.
[/list]

[h1]How to Use[/h1]
Open the relevant RimWorld tab and use the BPC buttons to create, select, or switch policies. Once a policy is active, adjust the normal RimWorld controls and BPC will save those settings to the preset you chose.

[h1]Compatibilities[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Supported RimWorld version:[/b] Current fork work is built for 1.6.
[*][b]Required mod:[/b] Harmony.
[*][b]Optional integrations:[/b] Work Tab, Animal Tab, Numbers, Combat Extended, Assign Animal Food, Children, school and learning, WeaponsTabReborn, Misc. Robots, Compositable Loadouts, Defensive Positions Forked, and Outfit Stands Plus Forked.
[*][b]Load order:[/b] Load after Harmony and the supported integration mods when applicable.
[/list]

[h1]Settings / Setup Instructions[/h1]
Configure policy defaults and integration options through RimWorld's mod settings and the in-game policy tabs.

When Outfit Stands Plus Forked is installed, BPC can trigger a pawn's assigned stand after an apparel policy change if the stand is reachable, usable, and safe to operate.
